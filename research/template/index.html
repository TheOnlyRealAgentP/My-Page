01

The question

Write: one or two sentences a first-year engineering student could follow. Start from something they have seen, such as a flooded road or a river gauge, then state the question your project answers.
  • Define every acronym the first time it appears.
  • Leave methods out of this section. That comes later.
02

Why it matters

Write: who benefits if this works, and what they can do that they couldn't before. One concrete example beats three general claims.
03

How it works

Write: the method in three to five steps, each one sentence. Pair it with one figure.
  • A diagram of the data flow works for software projects.
  • A map or plot works for modeling projects.
  • An interactive demo works best of all. Embed it in the figure slot below.
04

What we found

Write: the main result as a single sentence with a number in it, then up to three supporting points. Say what the result does not show, too. Students learn as much from the limits as from the finding.
