FIM & HAND reading list

Papers I keep coming back to while building FIMview: where HAND came from, how it became a national flood mapping method, how well it works, the tools, and what our lab has published on flood mapping.

Where HAND comes from

  1. Rennó, C. D., Nobre, A. D., Cuartas, L. A., Soares, J. V., Hodnett, M. G., Tomasella, J., et al. (2008). HAND, a new terrain descriptor using SRTM-DEM: Mapping terra-firme rainforest environments in Amazonia. Remote Sensing of Environment, 112(9), 3469-3481.

    The paper that introduced HAND: elevation measured from the nearest stream instead of from sea level. They built it from SRTM to map rainforest terrain in the Amazon.

  2. Nobre, A., Cuartas, L., Hodnett, M., Rennó, C., Rodrigues, G., Silveira, A., et al. (2011). Height Above the Nearest Drainage – a hydrologically relevant new terrain model. Journal of Hydrology, 404(1-2), 13-29.

    Makes the case that HAND tracks real hydrologic conditions on the ground, not just elevation.

  3. Nobre, A. D., Cuartas, L. A., Momo, M. R., Severo, D. L., Pinheiro, A., & Nobre, C. A. (2016). HAND contour: a new proxy predictor of inundation extent. Hydrological Processes, 30(2), 320-333.

    Uses HAND contours as a stand-in for flood extent. Pretty much every HAND flood map since builds on this idea.

HAND flood mapping for the whole country

  1. Maidment, D. R. (2017). Conceptual Framework for the National Flood Interoperability Experiment. JAWRA Journal of the American Water Resources Association, 53(2), 245-257.

    The framework behind the National Flood Interoperability Experiment, which set up using the National Water Model for flood forecasting nationwide.

  2. Liu, Y. Y., Maidment, D. R., Tarboton, D. G., Zheng, X., & Wang, S. (2018). A CyberGIS Integration and Computation Framework for High‐Resolution Continental‐Scale Flood Inundation Mapping. JAWRA Journal of the American Water Resources Association, 54(4), 770-784.

    A 10 m HAND raster for the entire lower 48, used for continental-scale flood mapping.

  3. Zheng, X., Tarboton, D. G., Maidment, D. R., Liu, Y. Y., & Passalacqua, P. (2018). River Channel Geometry and Rating Curve Estimation Using Height above the Nearest Drainage. JAWRA Journal of the American Water Resources Association, 54(4), 785-806.

    How to pull channel geometry and synthetic rating curves out of HAND, so a forecast flow becomes a water depth without surveyed cross sections.

  4. Zheng, X., Maidment, D. R., Tarboton, D. G., Liu, Y. Y., & Passalacqua, P. (2018). GeoFlood: Large‐Scale Flood Inundation Mapping Based on High‐Resolution Terrain Analysis. Water Resources Research, 54(12).

    GeoFlood: HAND flood mapping on high-resolution lidar terrain.

  5. Garousi‐Nejad, I., Tarboton, D. G., Aboutalebi, M., & Torres‐Rua, A. F. (2019). Terrain Analysis Enhancements to the Height Above Nearest Drainage Flood Inundation Mapping Method. Water Resources Research, 55(10), 7983-8009.

    Terrain analysis fixes that make HAND flood maps more accurate.

  6. Aristizabal, F., Salas, F., Petrochenkov, G., Grout, T., Avant, B., Bates, B., et al. (2023). Extending Height Above Nearest Drainage to Model Multiple Fluvial Sources in Flood Inundation Mapping Applications for the U.S. National Water Model. Water Resources Research, 59(5), e2022WR032039.

    NOAA's extension that lets HAND handle more than one river flooding the same spot. This is how OWP HAND-FIM does it.

How well it works

  1. Johnson, J. M., Munasinghe, D., Eyelade, D., & Cohen, S. (2019). An integrated evaluation of the National Water Model (NWM)–Height Above Nearest Drainage (HAND) flood mapping methodology. Natural Hazards and Earth System Sciences, 19(11), 2405-2420.

    Checks National Water Model + HAND flood maps against benchmark maps and breaks down where the errors come from.

  2. Godbout, L., Zheng, J. Y., Dey, S., Eyelade, D., Maidment, D., & Passalacqua, P. (2019). Error Assessment for Height Above the Nearest Drainage Inundation Mapping. JAWRA Journal of the American Water Resources Association, 55(4), 952-963.

    Where HAND inundation maps go wrong, and by how much.

  3. Afshari, S., Tavakoly, A. A., Rajib, M. A., Zheng, X., Follum, M. L., Omranian, E., et al. (2018). Comparison of new generation low-complexity flood inundation mapping tools with a hydrodynamic model. Journal of Hydrology, 556, 539-556.

    Compares low-complexity mapping tools, HAND among them, with a full hydrodynamic model.

  4. Li, Z., Duque, F. Q., Grout, T., Bates, B., & Demir, I. (2023). Comparative analysis of performance and mechanisms of flood inundation map generation using Height Above Nearest Drainage. Environmental Modelling & Software, 159, 105565.

    Compares how different HAND-based map generation methods perform, and why they differ.

Tools and code

  1. Baruah, A., Dhital, S., Cohen, S., Duc Tran, T. N., Elhaddad, H., Watts, C. L., et al. (2025). FIMserv v.1.0: A tool for streamlining Flood Inundation Mapping (FIM) using the United States operational hydrological forecasting framework. Environmental Modelling & Software, 192, 106581.

    FIMserv, the Python package FIMview is built on. It runs NOAA's operational HAND-FIM framework without the heavy setup.

  2. Dhital, S., Burauh, A., Nikrou, P., & Cohen, S. (2025). Enhancement of the NOAA Flood Inundation Mapping Framework (OWP HAND-FIM) through Surrogate Modeling. Preprint (ESS Open Archive).

    Speeds up and improves OWP HAND-FIM with surrogate modeling.

  3. Hu, A., & Demir, I. (2021). Real-Time Flood Mapping on Client-Side Web Systems Using HAND Model. Hydrology, 8(2), 65.

    HAND flood mapping running in the browser, in real time.

  4. Cohen, S., Brakenridge, G. R., Kettner, A., Bates, B., Nelson, J., McDonald, R., et al. (2018). Estimating Floodwater Depths from Flood Inundation Maps and Topography. JAWRA Journal of the American Water Resources Association, 54(4), 847-858.

    FwDET: gets flood depth from a flood extent map plus terrain.

  5. Follum, M. L., Tavakoly, A. A., Niemann, J. D., & Snow, A. D. (2017). AutoRAPID: A Model for Prompt Streamflow Estimation and Flood Inundation Mapping over Regional to Continental Extents. JAWRA Journal of the American Water Resources Association, 53(2), 280-299.

    AutoRAPID: couples river routing with flood mapping to get flows and flood extents fast over large areas.

  6. NOAA-OWP/inundation-mapping on GitHub

    NOAA's operational HAND-FIM code, set up for the National Water Model.

  7. sdmlua/FIMserv on GitHub

    FIMserv's source code.
