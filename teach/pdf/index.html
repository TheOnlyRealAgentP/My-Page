PDF tools

Turn photos, Word, Excel and PowerPoint files into PDFs, or put several PDFs together into one. Your files stay on your computer. Nothing gets uploaded.

Drop files here

    Good to know

    • Photos get one page each, turned the way your phone took them. Wide pictures get a sideways page.
    • Word, Excel and PowerPoint files become a picture of each page, so the text in the PDF can't be selected, and fonts this computer doesn't have get swapped for close ones. For an exact copy, use File, Save as, PDF in Office or File, Download, PDF in Google Docs.
    • Copy-protected PDFs go in as a picture of each page. A PDF that needs a password to open can't be used: open it, print it to a new PDF, and add that one.
    • Pages takes things like 1-3, 5 or 4- (page 4 to the end). Put a range backwards, like 5-1, to flip the order.
